# MineFleet AI 🚛⛏️

### Intelligent Mining Fleet & Route Optimisation Platform

**Smarter Routes. Faster Haulage. Optimised Operations.**

🔗 **Live Project:** [minefleet.ai.studio](https://minefleet.ai.studio)

MineFleet AI is an AI-powered mining fleet optimisation concept designed to improve the movement of dump trucks and haulage vehicles in mining operations. It aims to reduce avoidable waiting time, identify operational bottlenecks, optimise routes, and improve fleet utilisation through predictive analytics, intelligent dispatch recommendations, and digital twin simulation.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Mining operations often face challenges such as:

- 🚛 Inefficient truck allocation and haulage routes.
- ⏱️ Long queues at loading and unloading points.
- ⚙️ Uneven utilisation of trucks and loading facilities.
- ⛽ Unnecessary idling and potentially avoidable fuel consumption.
- 📊 Limited visibility into upcoming delays and operational bottlenecks.

These inefficiencies can increase operating costs and reduce material transportation productivity.

## 💡 Our Solution

MineFleet AI aims to transform traditional mining fleet coordination into a smarter, data-driven process.

The platform is designed to analyse operational conditions, predict potential delays, and recommend efficient routes and truck assignments. A digital twin simulation can help operators evaluate proposed changes before considering real-world implementation.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Fleet Monitoring** — Tracks truck status, location, routes, payload, and operational activities using available or simulated data.
2. **Bottleneck Detection** — Identifies congestion, queues, delays, and underutilised loading points.
3. **Predictive Analytics** — Estimates upcoming queue conditions and possible delays.
4. **Intelligent Routing** — Recommends suitable routes based on travel time, congestion, and route restrictions.
5. **Smart Dispatch** — Suggests truck assignments while considering compatibility and operational constraints.
6. **Digital Twin Simulation** — Compares baseline operations with proposed optimised scenarios.
7. **Performance Analysis** — Evaluates waiting time, turnaround time, fleet utilisation, material moved, and estimated fuel consumption.

## 🛠️ Proposed Technology Stack

The following technologies are candidates for implementation:

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Routing:** Graph-based shortest-path algorithms
- **Optimisation:** Constraint-based dispatch optimisation
- **Simulation:** Discrete-event simulation / custom digital twin
- **Analytics:** Python, Pandas, scikit-learn
- **Database:** SQLite for prototyping; PostgreSQL for future expansion
- **Visualisation:** Interactive maps, charts, and fleet dashboards

*The final stack will depend on the implemented prototype and project requirements.*
